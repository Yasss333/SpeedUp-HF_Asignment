# Speedup

Speedup is an AI email workspace for personal assistants and their clients. It helps them answer three questions at a glance: Who owns this email? What needs a reply first? What needs the client's approval?

An email can be read and still need action. Speedup gives those threads a visible place in the inbox, alongside separate queues for work the assistant can handle, decisions the client needs to make, and messages that are just FYI.

- **Personal assistant:** See urgent unanswered threads, review suggested replies, edit drafts, and escalate decisions to the client.
- **Client:** See what needs approval in a focused VIP view and record a decision.
- **Voice Copilot:** Ask what needs an urgent reply or approval. Vapi Copilot reads from a limited snapshot of the current view and has no permission to change email or approvals.

The demo uses sample inboxes and simulated incoming email. Drafts and approval decisions are saved in the workspace. Live inbox connections and email delivery are outside the demo.
